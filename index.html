IGOABROAD, study, work and live abroad

A dynamic, bilingual website with its own admin panel, built for I Go Abroad Inc, a study and work abroad consultancy in Dar es Salaam. The team edits pages, offers, events and promotions themselves and follows every enquiry from first contact to enrolment, without calling a developer.

Home page Hero, destinations bar and a free consultation button on every page.1 of 5

  • Admin panelStaff log in to manage enquiries, programmes, events, promotions, pages and site settings.
  • Enquiry managementEvery application is saved with its documents and moves through New, Contacted, In progress, Enrolled and Closed. Export to CSV.
  • Page builderNew pages are assembled from content blocks, in English and Swahili, without touching code.
  • English and SwahiliVisitors switch language at any time, and offers and pages are edited in both.
  • Course finderSearch programmes by subject, country and level, and import more with a CSV file.
  • Application with uploadsFour short steps. PDF and photo uploads are stored privately and open only for a logged-in admin.
  • Promotions and eventsScheduled banners with click counts, and an events page that feeds the homepage.
  • Visitor analyticsPage views, sources and button clicks, counted without cookies, inside the admin.
  • Chat assistantAnswers questions from the company's own information and hands over to WhatsApp.
  • Voice message and tourA recorded welcome and a short guided tour help first-time visitors find their way.
